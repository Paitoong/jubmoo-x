How to Play Gong Zhu

🎯 Objective

Avoid taking penalty cards (hearts and the Queen of Spades) while trying to collect bonus cards (Jack of Diamonds and 10 of Clubs).

📋 Card Values

  • ♠Q (Pig): -100
  • ♥A: -50, ♥K: -40, ♥Q: -30, ♥J: -20
  • ♥10,9,8,7,6,5: -10 each
  • ♥4,3,2: 0 (but still count as scoring cards)
  • ♦J (Sheep): +100
  • ♣10: +50 alone, or doubles all scoring cards

🎴 Exposing Cards

Before play begins, you may expose certain cards to double their value:

  • ♥A exposed: All hearts are worth double
  • ♠Q exposed: Pig is worth -200
  • ♦J exposed: Sheep is worth +200
  • ♣10 exposed: Quadruples instead of doubles

🃏 Gameplay

  • Play is anticlockwise
  • First hand: 2♠ holder leads
  • Must follow suit if possible
  • Highest card of led suit wins

🏆 Special Rules

  • Taking ALL hearts: +200 instead of -200
  • All hearts + Pig: Pig becomes +100
  • Game ends when someone reaches -1000
