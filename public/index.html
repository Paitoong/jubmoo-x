How to Play JubMoo

🎯 Objective

Avoid taking penalty cards (hearts and the Queen of Spades) while trying to collect bonus cards (Jack of Diamonds and 10 of Clubs).

📋 Card Values

  • ♠Q (Pig): -100
  • ♥A: -50, ♥K: -40, ♥Q: -30, ♥J: -20
  • ♥10: -10, ♥9: -9, ♥8: -8, ♥7: -7, ♥6: -6, ♥5: -5
  • ♥4: -4, ♥3: -3, ♥2: -2
  • ♦J (Sheep): +100 (or -100 if another player takes all hearts)
  • ♣10: +50 alone, or doubles all scoring cards

Gameplay

  • Play is anticlockwise
  • First hand: 2♠ holder leads
  • Must follow suit if possible
  • Highest card of led suit wins

🏆 Special Rules

  • Taking ALL hearts: +194 instead of -194
  • All hearts + Pig: Pig becomes +100 (total +294)
  • ♦J = -100 if taken by another player when someone has all hearts
  • All hearts + Pig + Sheep + ♣10 = +1000
  • Game ends when someone reaches the target score
